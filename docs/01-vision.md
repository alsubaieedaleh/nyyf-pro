# 01 · Vision

## The problem

A Saudi real estate broker answers the same three questions all day: *What is this property worth? What has sold nearby, and for how much? Who do I have that wants it?* Today the answers are scattered across Ministry of Justice deal data, Aqar listings, WhatsApp groups, Excel sheets, and memory. Pricing a listing or convincing a client takes hours and is often a guess.

## The product

**Nyyf Pro is the broker's map-first workspace.** Open it and you see the market: every district coloured by price, every recent deal and live listing as a dot, all moving smoothly as you pan and zoom. Tap anywhere to get an instant valuation backed by real comparables. Save it to a client, put it in a deal, and share a branded report in one tap.

## Positioning

- **For** licensed real estate brokers and small brokerage offices in Saudi Arabia (Riyadh first).
- **Who** need to price properties, find opportunities, and win client trust quickly.
- **Nyyf Pro is** a map-first market intelligence and brokerage workspace.
- **Unlike** listing portals (built for buyers), data platforms such as Suhail (built for looking things up), or generic CRMs (no market data), Nyyf Pro puts market data, valuation, inventory, and clients in one place built for the broker's day.
- **Map quality bar:** Suhail (suhail.ai), the reference the owner chose. See 05.

## Product principles

1. **The map is the home screen.** Every feature should be reachable from, or visible on, the map.
2. **Simple first, deep on demand.** The first view shows one clear thing (price per m²). Detail appears only when the user asks for it (tap, drawer, filter).
3. **Smooth or it does not ship.** 60 fps pan and zoom, no layout jumps, no full-screen spinners. Perceived speed is a feature.
4. **Every number is explainable.** A valuation always comes with its range, its comparables, and the date of the data.
5. **Arabic first, English equal.** RTL by default, numerals and units localised, full English parity.
6. **Mobile in the field, desktop in the office.** The same workflows work on a phone held in one hand and on a wide screen.
7. **The broker owns their data.** Clients, notes, and listings are private to the broker or their office.

## Success metrics (first 6 months after public launch)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Weekly active brokers | 500 |
| Valuations run per active broker per week | ≥ 10 |
| Reports shared to clients per active broker per week | ≥ 3 |
| Time to first meaningful map paint (4G phone) | < 1.5 s |
| Map interaction frame rate | ≥ 55 fps median |
| Valuation median absolute error vs. actual deal price (held-out) | ≤ 12% |
| Week-4 retention of new brokers | ≥ 40% |

## Non-goals (for now)

- Becoming a consumer listing portal competing with Aqar or Bayut.
- Processing payments, escrow, or contracts (Ejar integration is a later consideration).
- Covering all Saudi cities at launch. Riyadh first, then Jeddah, Dammam/Khobar, Makkah, Madinah.

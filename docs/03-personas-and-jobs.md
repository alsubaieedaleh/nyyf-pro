# 03 · Personas and jobs to be done

## Primary personas

### 1. Faisal, the independent broker (core user)
- Licensed (FAL) individual broker in Riyadh, 3 to 10 years in the market. Works from his phone, mostly in the car and on site.
- Deals: land and villas, 2 to 6 closings a month. Lives in WhatsApp.
- Pain: pricing a new listing takes calls to colleagues; clients do not trust his number; loses track of who wanted what.
- Wins with Nyyf Pro when: he can stand on a plot, tap the map, and send the owner a branded valuation with comparables in under a minute.

### 2. Noura, the brokerage office manager
- Runs an office of 5 to 25 agents. Works on a laptop.
- Pain: no shared view of the office's listings and deals; inconsistent pricing between agents; no market reports to win exclusive mandates.
- Wins when: she sees the whole office's inventory and pipeline on one map, assigns leads, and produces a district market report for a developer pitch.

### 3. Khalid, the land and investment specialist
- Focus on raw land, schemes (مخططات), and commercial plots. Heavy data user, desktop.
- Pain: needs deal history by scheme and street, price trends, and supply (offers) vs. sold prices to find under-priced land.
- Wins when: he draws an area on the map and instantly gets volume, median price per m², trend, and the gap between asking and sold prices.

## Secondary personas
- **Property appraiser (مقيم)**: uses comparables and the valuation engine as a starting point. Later, possibly a paid tier.
- **Broker's client (owner or buyer)**: never logs in; receives shared reports and listing pages. Their experience must be beautiful and mobile-friendly because it is the broker's marketing.

## Jobs to be done (ranked)

| # | When I... | I want to... | So I can... |
|---|---|---|---|
| J1 | get a new property from an owner | price it fast and defensibly | win the mandate and set a realistic asking price |
| J2 | talk to a client about an area | show what sold nearby, when, and for how much | earn trust with evidence, not opinion |
| J3 | hunt for opportunities | spot listings priced below the market | bring deals to investors first |
| J4 | have many listings and clients | match each listing to the clients who want it | close faster without forgetting anyone |
| J5 | send something to a client | share a clean branded report or listing page | look professional and get forwarded |
| J6 | run an office | see the team's inventory, pipeline, and performance | manage and grow the business |
| J7 | follow a market | get alerted when a district's prices or volume move | advise clients before competitors do |
